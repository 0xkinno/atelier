How it works

From connect to first sale in under sixty seconds

01
🔒
Connect wallet
Open inside Nimiq Pay and sign in with your wallet. No email, no password, no account to create.
02
🎨
Build your storefront
Pick a name, add a bio and avatar, choose your accent color. Your storefront is live at atelier.app/you.
03
📦
Upload your work
Add products with a title, preview image, price in any fiat currency, and the downloadable file. Unlimited products.

Share your link. Buyers pay in NIM or USDT, verified on-chain, and get instant file access. You keep 100%.
